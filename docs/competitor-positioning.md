# Competitor Positioning

## Competitive Set Reviewed

- Paco Collective
- COACT
- Legacy Marketing
- Gradient Experience
- EVOKE Experiences

## Messaging Gap

Most competitor agencies emphasize scale, activations, and polished experiential work. Cake Marketing Collective is strongest when positioned around:

- founder-led creative direction
- culturally fluent audience building
- sponsorship development for community-centered events
- art, justice, and place-based storytelling
- collaborative, collective-led production instead of generic agency language

## Copy Direction

- Lead with culture, memory, and community power before broad "marketing agency" terms.
- Keep service descriptions outcome-driven and event-specific.
- Use the founder page to strengthen E-E-A-T signals.
- Treat sponsorship development as a signature offering rather than a secondary bullet.

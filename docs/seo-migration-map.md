# SEO Migration Map

## Primary Positioning

Cake Marketing Collective should rank around culturally fluent event marketing, sponsorship development, community-centered brand experiences, and founder-led creative direction.

## Page Targets

| Route | Primary Intent | Supporting Topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Event marketing collective | cultural events, brand experiences, sponsorship support |
| `/about` | Cake Douglas founder story | creative direction, cultural curation, community strategy |
| `/services` | Event branding and sponsorship services | experiential design, social strategy, project management |
| `/portfolio` | Event marketing portfolio | LinkFest, collaborations, audience engagement outcomes |
| `/work-with-us` | Discovery call and intake | consultation, partnerships, event planning inquiry |

## Metadata Priorities

- Unique page titles and descriptions for every route.
- Self-referencing canonicals on the apex domain.
- Organization, Person, ProfessionalService, and FAQ schema.
- XML sitemap and crawlable `robots.txt`.
- Rich social share images for homepage and portfolio.

## Post-Launch Tasks

- Submit sitemap in Google Search Console.
- Inspect coverage and manual actions after launch.
- Add local SEO elements only after business address and NAP are confirmed.
